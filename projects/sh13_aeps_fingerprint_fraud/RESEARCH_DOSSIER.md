## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & INVESTIGATIVE THESIS

Thousands of everyday citizens in India have experienced sudden, unauthorized money debits from their bank accounts with **zero OTP sent, zero SMS alerts, zero debit card swipes, and zero phishing links clicked**. 

The vulnerability lies in the **Aadhaar-Enabled Payment System (AePS)** coupled with public, unmasked document availability on **State Land Revenue & Registration Portals** (such as Bihar IGRS, UP Bhulekh, Haryana Jamabandi, Rajasthan E-Dharti).

Syndicates in Bihar (Nawada, Nalanda, Gaya), Haryana (Gurugram, Palwal), Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal have systematically exploited this loop:
1. They download publicly hosted property sale deeds containing high-resolution thumbprints and 12-digit Aadhaar numbers.
2. They print the inverted thumb impressions on butter paper using graphic design software.
3. They use rubber stamp-making machines (poly stampers) and liquid photopolymer/silicone gel to manufacture physical, artificial silicone fingers with exact ridge patterns.
4. Using micro-ATMs or rogue Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent) IDs, they trigger AePS cash withdrawals of ₹10,000 (maximum per-transaction limit) without needing an OTP or victim presence.

## 2. MODUS OPERANDI BREAKDOWN (THE 4-STAGE PIPELINE)

### Stage 1: Public Document Scraping
- State land registry websites require property buyers and sellers to provide biometric thumbprints on registered sale deeds and mutation records.
- Due to lack of data masking, these scanned legal deeds—often 10 to 20 pages in PDF format—are freely accessible or searchable by plot/deed number.
- Scammers extract the buyer/seller's Aadhaar card copy, PAN number, and clean 300+ DPI thumbprint impression.

### Stage 2: Biometric Fingerprint Cloning
- The extracted fingerprint is converted to negative polarity in Photoshop or CorelDraw.
- Printed using laser printer onto tracing / butter paper.
- Placed inside a poly-stamper / UV curing chamber with liquid silicone or photopolymer gel.
- Cured in 15–20 minutes to produce a flexible, reusable 3D rubber thumb clone matching human dermal ridges.

### Stage 3: The AePS Cash Extraction
- AePS was engineered by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) for financial inclusion in rural areas where debit cards and smartphones are rare.
- **The Core Vulnerability:** AePS requires only:
  1. Aadhaar Number
  2. Bank Name
  3. Fingerprint Biometric
- **Zero Two-Factor Authentication:** No OTP is required from the customer.
- Scammers use rogue POS machines or fake Business Correspondent (BC) credentials registered with third-party fintech companies.
- Placing the silicone clone on older **L0 optical biometric scanners** (Mantra MFS100, Morpho MSO 1300) tricks the scanner into verifying the print as genuine living tissue.
- The scammer withdraws the maximum limit (₹10,000 per transaction) or transfers it into mule e-wallets.

### Stage 4: Money Laundering & Cash Out
- Cash siphoned from AePS accounts is funneled through layers of bank accounts and crypto/UPI mule accounts, leaving victims with zero initial recourse until they check their passbooks.

## 4. SYSTEMIC REFORMS INITIATED

1. **State Land Registry Masking:** MHA and I4C issued strict directives to state registration departments to black out/mask biometric thumbprints from all downloadable deeds.
2. **Mandatory L1 Scanner Migration:** Phasing out legacy L0 scanners in favor of L1 scanners with certified hardware cryptographic security and anti-spoof liveness detection.
3. **Operator Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** NPCI implemented mandatory Aadhaar-based biometric 2FA for business correspondents every 3 hours or before executing transactions.
4. **Biometric Lock Mechanism:** UIDAI's instantaneous lock feature that prevents all authentication attempts until unlocked by the citizen.

## 5. CITIZEN ARMOR: HOW TO IMMUNIZE YOURSELF IN 60 SECONDS

1. Open the **mAadhaar App** or login to `myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in` with OTP.
2. Navigate to **Lock / Unlock Biometrics**.
3. Toggle the status to **LOCKED**.
4. Result: Any micro-ATM or AePS machine attempting to authenticate with your Aadhaar will throw an immediate error: `Error Code 330: Biometrics are locked by Aadhaar holder`.
