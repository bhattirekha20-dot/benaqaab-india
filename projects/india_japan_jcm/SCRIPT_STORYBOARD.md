# Script and Storyboard — India–Japan JCM

## Recommended title

**India–Japan Carbon Credit Mechanism: Climate Finance Ya Carbon Accounting?**

## Hook options

1. **Recommended:** Japan India mein paise lagayega, India emission cuts karega — aur carbon credits dono countries ke climate targets mein count ho sakte hain. Yeh climate finance hai, ya carbon accounting ka naya risk?
2. India aur Japan ne carbon credits ka system bana diya. Lekin ek tonne emission cut ko do baar count hone se kaun rokega?
3. Agar Japan India ke clean projects ko fund kare, toh credit kis desh ka hoga?

## Final Hinglish narration

Japan India mein clean technology aur investment la sakta hai, India greenhouse-gas emissions kam karega. Par India–Japan carbon credit mechanism mein credit kisey milega — aur kya ek hi emission cut do baar count ho sakta hai?

Iska naam hai Joint Crediting Mechanism, ya JCM. India aur Japan ne iske liye August 2025 mein cooperation agreement sign kiya. June 2026 mein Rules of Implementation adopt hue. Yeh Paris Agreement ke Article 6.2 ke under aata hai.

Simple language mein mechanism aise kaam karta hai. Japan ya Japanese companies India mein kisi mitigation project ke saath cooperate karte hain. Project ke emissions ko ek conservative reference level se compare kiya jaata hai. Difference, agar independently verify ho, JCM credit ban sakta hai.

Lekin credit automatic nahi milta. Joint Committee project approve karti hai. Third-party entity emission reduction verify karti hai. Dono governments registries maintain karti hain. Aur corresponding adjustment ka rule ensure karta hai ki ek hi reduction India aur Japan dono apne climate target mein double-count na kar dein.

Rules ke mutabik crediting period maximum ten years ka ho sakta hai. Kuch authorised credits Japan ke NDC ke liye internationally transfer bhi ho sakte hain — lekin har Indian emission cut automatically Japanese credit nahi ban jaata.

Benefit kya hai? India ko low-carbon technology, investment aur capacity-building mil sakti hai. Risk kya hai? Weak baseline, poor monitoring ya double counting se credit ki credibility kharab ho sakti hai.

Isliye JCM ko sirf carbon trading mat samjhiye. Yeh ek test hai: kya international money se India mein real clean projects banenge, aur unka accounting transparent rahega?

Aapko kya lagta hai — climate finance, ya carbon accounting ka risk? Comment kijiye. Aise evidence-led explainers ke liye Benaqaab India ko subscribe kijiye.

## Visual rules

- Full-screen 1080×1920 AI illustrations and original motion graphics.
- Do not use real politician faces or fabricated project photographs.
- Use `AI ILLUSTRATIVE VISUAL` for generated scenes.
- Use primary-source fact cards for the dates and rules.
- Keep captions large and compact, maximum five or six words per line.
- Use one dominant cyan/gold directional motion line to represent a credit moving through the system.
