## Title

India–Japan Carbon Credits: Credit Kisey Milega? | Article 6.2 Explained

## Description

India aur Japan ka Joint Crediting Mechanism, ya JCM, simple language mein samjhiye.

Japan ya Japanese entities India ke low-carbon mitigation projects ke saath cooperate kar sakte hain. Project emissions ko ek conservative reference level se compare kiya jaata hai. Verified reduction se JCM credit ban sakta hai — lekin credit automatic nahi milta.

Is Short mein:
- Paris Agreement ka Article 6.2
- Reference emissions minus project emissions
- Joint Committee approval
- Third-party validation aur verification
- Credit sharing
- Registries aur corresponding adjustments
- Double counting se bachne ka mechanism
- Maximum 10-year crediting period

Important: JCM ko India ke pollution rights bechne ke simple model ke roop mein na samjhein. Official rules project approval, verification, authorisation and accounting safeguards require karte hain. Future investment ya project outcomes ko guaranteed result ke roop mein present nahi kiya gaya hai.
