In this comprehensive investigative documentary (Episode EP-19), Benaqaab India breaks down the multi-trillion dollar economy using primary institutional data from the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), World Bank, and IMF.

Hum deconstruct karenge:
1. Per-Capita Income Trap: India Total GDP mein 5th rank par hai, lekin Per-Capita Income mein 136th rank ($2,730) par kyun?
2. K-Shaped Economy: Top 10% ke liye luxury SUVs aur penthouses ka boom, jabki small cars (-42%) aur rural two-wheelers ki sales mein girawat.
3. RBI Red Flag: Net Household Savings 50 saal ke sabse nichle star (5.3% of GDP) par kyun crash hui, aur parivaron par karz 45.8% record level par kaise pahuncha?
4. The Missing Factory: Manufacturing share 30 saal se 15.8% par atka kyun hai, jabki China 27.4% factories ke dum par ameer bana?
5. The 2045 Demographic Clock: 1.1 crore yuva har saal job market mein aa rahe hain — kya Bharat ameer banne se pehle budha ho jayega?
