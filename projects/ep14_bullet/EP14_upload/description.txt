Bullet Train 2027: 50 Km Pehle, 508 Km Poora | Mumbai-Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail

2027 mein India ki pehli bullet train chalu hone wali hai — pehla hissa Surat se Bilimora, sirf 50 kilometre. Lekin asli kahaani 508 kilometre ki hai. Is video mein: corridor kitna bada hai, abhi kitna ban chuka hai, Mumbai wala mushkil hissa (21 km tunnel, 7 km samundar ke neeche) kya hai, kaun si train chalegi, aur paisa kahan se aa raha hai. Aakhir mein: poora schedule aur kaun si cheezein dekhni chahiye.
