### 2. ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund — confirmed Cabinet decision
- PIB posted the Cabinet release on 6 October 2026 at 3:12 PM.
- The Union Cabinet approved a Government of India commitment of ₹10,000 crore toward an SME Growth Fund.
- The official description says it is intended to provide direct/patient equity capital to high-potential growth-oriented SMEs, with a major focus on manufacturing and consideration of industrial clusters in Tier-II and Tier-III cities.
- Do not claim that money has already reached individual companies.
- Primary source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2319532&reg=48&lang=1
- PIB factsheet: https://pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=151082&NoteId=151082&ModuleId=16&reg=48&lang=1

### 3. Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority — confirmed Cabinet decision
- PIB factsheet posted 6 October 2026 at 19:09.
- ITLA will serve as a specialised mechanism for research, planning, project appraisal, monitoring and impact assessment across transport and logistics.
- Its stated plan includes a 10-year-or-more National Transport Master Plan, appraisal of Government of India infrastructure projects costing ₹500 crore or more, and an integrated data repository drawing on systems such as GSTN e-way bills, FASTag, Vahan and GPS-based systems.
- Primary source: https://pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=151083&NoteId=151083&ModuleId=16&reg=48&lang=1
- Referenced Cabinet release: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2319529&reg=48&lang=1
