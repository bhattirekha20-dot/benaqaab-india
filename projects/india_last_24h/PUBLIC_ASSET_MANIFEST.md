## Video policy

- The official BCCI highlight page is included as a linked source and its official thumbnail is used in the preview.
- Rahul Gandhi protest video candidates from NDTV, India Today and Congress/AICC have been audited in `PUBLIC_VIDEO_AUDIT.md`.
- No television-news footage or downloaded third-party clip is embedded without a clear reuse licence.
- For future Shorts, use official or explicitly licensed video when the source permits reuse; otherwise use a source-linked video card, public data, official still and original motion graphics.
- When a public clip is permission-cleared, apply the user's warm sepia/orange grade with a dark-green horizontal band using `apply_public_video_grade.py`.
- “Publicly available” is not automatically the same as “public domain.” Keep visible attribution and respect the source platform’s terms.
