# Script and Storyboard — India in the Last 24 Hours

## Working title

**India Last 24 Hours: Vote Row, ₹10,000 Crore, Earthquake & Cricket**

## 0:00 hook question

**“India ke pichhle 24 ghante mein vote row, ₹10,000 crore, earthquake aur cricket — sabse important kya badla?”**

## Final Hinglish narration

India ke pichhle 24 ghante mein vote row, 10,000 crore, earthquake aur cricket — sabse important kya badla? Yeh hai 7 October 2026 ka verified India roundup.

Sabse pehle democracy. 6 October ko political parties ne Election Commission ki taraf march kiya. ECI ke official statement ke mutabik, shaam 4 baje tak appointment request nahi aayi thi. Uske baad Rahul Gandhi ke leadership mein 240 MPs ka delegation milna chahta tha. ECI ne kaha ki protocol ke mutabik meeting Commission ke premises par honi chahiye. Political dispute aur voter-roll revision ke objections abhi ongoing hain — final verdict nahi.

Economy mein Cabinet ne 10,000 crore rupaye ke SME Growth Fund ko approve kiya. Plan growth-oriented small and medium businesses ko equity capital dene ka hai — especially manufacturing aur Tier Two, Tier Three clusters ko. Paise individual companies tak pahunch gaye hain, aisa abhi claim nahi hai.

Infrastructure mein Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority, ya ITLA, approve hua. Iska kaam transport planning, project appraisal aur monitoring ko integrate karna hai. Proposal mein 10-year-plus transport master plan aur 500 crore rupaye ya usse bade projects ki appraisal shamil hai.

Defence update: Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit 31 October se India ke 29th Chief of the Air Staff honge.

Enforcement mein DRI ne Cooch Behar mein lagbhag 8.3 kilogram foreign-origin gold, around 12.37 crore rupaye value ka, seize kiya; chaar arrests hue. Investigation abhi chal rahi hai.

Earthquake alert: National Center for Seismology ne Chamoli, Uttarakhand mein 6 October raat 10:23 par magnitude 4.9 event record kiya. IMD ne Jammu-Kashmir, Himachal, Uttarakhand aur northwest ke kuch areas mein rain, thunderstorm aur gusty winds ka forecast diya hai. Local advisories check kijiye.

Sports mein India ne Lucknow mein West Indies ko 8 wickets se haraaya — 171 ka target 14.4 overs mein chase hua.

Aur yaad rakhiye: khabar tez ho sakti hai, par sach ki speed source se aati hai.

**Benaqaab India — shor nahi, source ke saath.**

## Visual / rights plan

- Use official PIB source images for the SME, ITLA, Air Chief and DRI stories with source attribution on the relevant card and in the description.
- Use two public ECI/protest images for the democracy item, with visible credit to the source article and Congress image credit.
- Use official NCS, IMD and BCCI data as recreated source cards, never as fabricated screenshots.
- Use the official BCCI video thumbnail and link the official highlights page; do not silently copy a third-party broadcast clip.
- Use AI-generated illustrations only where a suitable public source image is unavailable or where an explanatory background is needed. Label them clearly.
- Keep each text panel within safe margins. No panel may cover a source image caption or narration detail.
