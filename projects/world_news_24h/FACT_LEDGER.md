Edition: 7 October 2026. This is a fixed, dated editorial snapshot, not live news.

## Defined coverage window
6 October 2026 00:00 IST through 7 October 2026 00:00 IST. Publication and reporting dates are checked against that edition window rather than a moving “right now” claim. Stories may concern earlier events whose result or announcement was reported during the window. This is not exhaustive worldwide coverage.
