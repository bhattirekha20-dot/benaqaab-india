Made-in-India Chips: 5 of 12 Live, Dholera Ka Intezaar | India Semiconductor Mission

India ke chip safar ki asli picture. September 2026 tak 12 approved units mein se 5 commercial production kar rahi hain — lekin wo sab packaging aur testing units hain. Chip banane wala pehla bada silicon fab Dholera mein ban raha hai: 91 hazaar crore, 28 se 110 nanometer, aur commercial output ki khabrein mid-2028 ki hain. Saath hi: Semicon 2.0 ka 1.27 lakh crore package, Odisha ka pehla silicon carbide fab — aur sabse bada sawaal: phir bhi India har saal 30 billion dollar ke chip kyun import karta hai?
