### 15. 🔬 Why Bay of Bengal Gets ~4× More Cyclones Than Arabian Sea
* **Hook:** *"Duniya ke 10% cyclone India me aate hain — par 4 me se 3 Bengal ki taraf. Kyun?"*  
* **Angle:** The physical oceanography and meteorology: Sea Surface Temperature (SST 28–30°C), three-sided basin heat trapping, huge freshwater discharge from Ganga-Brahmaputra forming a low-salinity surface lid that prevents vertical cooling, vs Arabian Sea upwelling and high wind shear.
* **Verified Data:** Subcontinent's 1,019 disturbances: 890 on East Coast, 260 Odisha landfalls. (Counter-trend: Arabian Sea is warming faster in recent years).
* **Sources:** [India Water Portal / IMD Archive](https://indiawaterportal.org/climate-change/disasters/faq-on-tropical-cyclones-in-india-2).

### 17. 🔬 Why Delhi's Air Gets Trapped Every Winter (The Invisible Lid)
* **Hook:** *"Delhi ki hawa gandi hoti hai — par winter me wo jaati kyun nahi? Jawab zameen se 500 metre upar hai."*  
* **Angle:** Temperature Inversion & Mixing Height collapse. Warmer air aloft acts as an atmospheric lid. Delhi’s bowl topography between Himalayas and Aravallis prevents horizontal dispersion.
* **Verified Data:** Summer mixing height 1–2 km collapses to 200–500m in winter. Surface at 18°C while 500m aloft is 25°C. WHO PM2.5 standard 5 µg/m³ vs Delhi winter peaks 350–450 µg/m³.
* **Sources:** [IITM Basin Studies](https://www.tropmet.res.in), [CPCB Air Quality Data](https://cpcb.nic.in).

### 18. 🔬 Why Your Flight Home is 2 Hours Longer (The Jet Stream Myth)
* **Hook:** *"Delhi se New York ki flight, aur wapsi — distance same, time nahi. Aur ye Earth ke ghoomne ki wajah se nahi hai."*  
* **Angle:** Myth-buster: Earth's rotation does NOT directly shorten flights (atmosphere moves with the planet). The actual driver is the high-altitude tropospheric **Jet Stream** (80–140 mph, winter 200+ mph).
* **The Math:** 500 mph airspeed + 120 mph tailwind = 620 mph ground speed eastbound; 500 - 120 = 380 mph westbound.

### 19. 🔬 Chandrayaan-3 ChaSTE: Moon’s Extreme Soil Gradient
* **Hook:** *"Surface pe +50°C. Sirf 8 cm neeche -10°C. Ye ISRO ke ChaSTE ne naapa — lunar south pole ka pehla temperature profile."*  
* **Angle:** Chandra’s Surface Thermophysical Experiment probe with 10 thermal sensors penetrating 10 cm into lunar regolith. Explains why lunar regolith is the ultimate thermal insulator.
* **Verified Data:** +40°C to +50°C at top surface dropping sharply to -10°C at 8 cm depth. Permanently shadowed craters sit at 40 Kelvin (-233°C).
* **Sources:** [ISRO ChaSTE First Results Release](https://www.isro.gov.in).

### 20. 🔬 Lakshadweep Corals: What Do the Numbers Actually Say?
* **Hook:** *"Ek hi reef ke liye teen studies — 82%, 73%, 31%. Kaun sa number sach hai? Teeno — aur yahi sabse bada saboot hai."*
