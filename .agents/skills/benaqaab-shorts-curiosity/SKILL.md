# 🎯 Benaqaab Shorts Curiosity & First-Second Retention Engine

## 1. The 3-Layer Frame-0 Hook
Every YouTube Short must answer "Why am I watching this?" in the first second (Frame 0):
1. **Layer 1: Visual Intrigue (Eyes)** — An unexpected, arresting, or bizarre visual state on screen at 0.00s. Never start on a blank screen or a slow fade-in.
2. **Layer 2: Verbal Hook (Ears)** — A punchy, spoken first line in Hinglish that opens a high-stakes curiosity loop.
3. **Layer 3: Sharpener Text (Brain)** — 2 to 4 bold words on screen that reinforce the hook and prevent swipe-away without duplicating the voiceover word-for-word.

## 2. Kallaway's 6 Hook Archetypes
- **The Negative Revelation:** "Yeh 1 galti aapka pura bank account khali kar sakti hai..."
- **The Secret / Hidden Mechanism:** "Government is feature ko public se kyu chupana chahti hai?"
- **The Extreme Contrast / Paradox:** "Duniya ka sabse mehnga bridge, par train nahi chal sakti?"
- **The High-Stakes Countdown:** "Sirf 48 ghante bache hain is naye rule se pehle..."
- **The Forensic Investigation:** "Maine is viral video ki original file inspect ki, aur sach kuch aur nikla..."
- **The Counter-Intuitive Truth:** "Fat kam karne ke liye ghee khana zaroori hai?"

## 3. The Chenab Bridge Minimal Fullscreen Layout
- **Visual Area:** 65%–75% of the screen remains completely unobstructed, showcasing cinematic imagery, map graphics, or forensic evidence.
- **Top Card:** Single minimal translucent dark glass floating card (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`, top accent bar: `#f97316` brand orange). Contains clean 2–3 word summary cells.
- **Top-Right:** Micro circular channel logo.
- **Bottom-Left Captions:** Compact 2-word maximum caption pill (e.g. `chal sakti`) with orange `#f97316` active word highlight.
- **Presenter / Avatar:** Maximum 1–2 seconds on screen. Never let host faces block evidence or motion.

## 4. Retention & Pacing Directives
- **Visual Velocity:** Change the visual framing, angle, or graphic element every 2.0 to 3.5 seconds.
- **Curiosity Loops:** Open loop in seconds 0–3, escalate stakes in seconds 10–25, introduce a twist or key evidence in seconds 25–40, resolve and deliver the payoff at seconds 42–48.
- **Duration:** Under 50 seconds strictly.
