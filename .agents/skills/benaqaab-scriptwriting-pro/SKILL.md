# ✍️ BENAQAAB SCRIPTWRITING PRO — THE RETENTION SCRIPT ENGINE

This skill governs the creation of all scripts for the **Benaqaab India** channel (both vertical Shorts and long-form investigative documentaries).

---

## 🚫 1. THE ANTI-AI SCRIPT SLOP MANIFESTO

Generic AI scripts sound like corporate press releases or translated Wikipedia articles. They are **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**.

### ❌ Banned Cliches & AI Tells:
- *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
- *"Have you ever wondered why..."*
- *"Let us dive deep into the fascinating world of..."*
- *"Make sure to watch till the end to find out..."*
- *"Cybercrime is a growing menace affecting millions..."*
- Formal, stilted Hindi: *"Yeh ek aisi prakriya hai jisme...", "Hamare desh mein tatkal prabhav se..."*
- Passive voice without personal stakes: *"Complaints were filed and action was taken..."*

### ✅ Mandatory Conversational Energy:
- **Intimate Second-Person ("Aap"):** Speak directly to the viewer as if sharing a confidential forensic file over coffee.
- **Relatable Vulnerability:** Make the viewer feel: *"Yeh kal subah mere ya mere parents ke saath ho sakta hai!"*
- **Colloquial Hinglish Cadence:** Use dynamic transition pivots:
  - *"Imagine karo..."*
  - *"Sochiye zara..."*
  - *"Lekin asli twist yahan shuru hota hai..."*
  - *"Ab sabse bada jhatka..."*
  - *"Yeh sunte hi aap bologe — aisa kaise ho sakta hai?"*
  - *"Sach sun kar aapke pairon tale zameen khisak jayegi."*

## 🎯 2. THE 5-ACT SHORT SCRIPT ARCHITECTURE (STRICTLY < 45 SECONDS)

For YouTube Shorts, the script MUST fit within **110 to 125 spoken words** at a natural high-energy rate (+24% to +28% TTS speed), structured in 5 causal acts:

```
[0s — 8s]   ACT 1: THE INCITING TRAP (In-Medias-Res Hook)
[8s — 17s]  ACT 2: THE SMASH (The Legal Truth & Forensics)
[17s — 26s] ACT 3: THE SHADOW SYNDICATE (The Hidden Mechanism)
[26s — 34s] ACT 4: THE HARD NUMBERS (Two-Source Verified Scale)
[34s — 43s] ACT 5: THE ARMOR & IDENTITY (Golden Hour Defense + Outro CTA)
```

### Breakdown by Act:

#### Act 1: The Inciting Trap (0.00s – 8.00s | ~22 words)
- **Goal:** Stop the thumb in 1.5 seconds.
- **Rule:** Plunge directly into the dialogue or situation. Re-enact the scammer's threat or the alarming event.
- **Template:** *"Imagine karo: aapke phone par ek video call aati hai. Samne CBI officer hai aur bolta hai — 'Aap agle 72 ghante tak Digital Arrest hain!' Par sach sun kar aapke hosh udd jayenge..."*

#### Act 2: The Smash (8.00s – 17.00s | ~25 words)
- **Goal:** Demolish the lie with undeniable legal/scientific authority.
- **Rule:** Deliver the **Sticky Soundbite** — the single line that viewers will quote to their family.
- **Template:** *"Indian law mein 'Digital Arrest' jaisa koi kanoon exist hi nahi karta! Supreme Court ne saaf kaha hai — yeh kanoon nahi, Digital Dacoity hai! Kyunki asli police darwaza tod kar aati hai, video call par nahi!"*

#### Act 3: The Shadow Syndicate (17.00s – 26.00s | ~24 words)
- **Goal:** The Onion Reveal — peel back layer 2 to expose the global or systemic puppet masters.
- **Rule:** Answer where the calls/frauds actually come from. Contrast expectation with shocking reality.
- **Template:** *"Lekin sabse bada twist: Yeh calls kisi police station se nahi, Cambodia aur Myanmar ke jungle compounds se aati hain! Jahan majboor Indian IT workers ko qaid karke yeh scam chalwaya ja raha hai!"*

#### Act 4: The Hard Numbers (26.00s – 34.00s | ~22 words)
- **Goal:** Prove the immense scale using conservative, two-source verified official figures.
- **Rule:** State money, complaints, or rescue numbers with dramatic weight.
- **Template:** *"Ab tak 3,000 crore se zyada loota ja chuka hai! MEA ne 1,100 se zyada bandi Indians ko rescue kiya hai aur hazaron Skype accounts ban hue hain!"*

#### Act 5: The Armor & Identity (34.00s – 43.00s | ~24 words)
- **Goal:** Give the viewer concrete, life-saving armor and lock the channel identity.
- **Rule:** The 60-second action protocol (1930 / official portals) + clear Like/Subscribe verbal CTA.
- **Template:** *"Yaad rakhiye: aisi call aate hi turant kaato aur 1930 pe report karo. Aise real investigative sach ke liye — Benaqaab India ko subscribe karo!"*

## 🎙️ 3. VOICE ENGINE & PHONETIC CALIBRATION

Edge-TTS neural voices require phonetic spelling for natural, human inflection without awkward pauses:

1. **Phone Numbers & Helplines:**
   - ❌ Do NOT write: `1930` (TTS reads as "one thousand nine hundred thirty" or pauses).
   - ✅ DO write: `unnees sau tees` or `1930` with explicit phonetic context.
2. **Currencies & Large Numbers:**
   - ❌ Do NOT write: `₹55,050 Cr` in the VO text.
   - ✅ DO write: `pachpan hazaar crore rupaye` in VO (keep `₹55,050 Cr` for on-screen text).
3. **Acronyms:**
   - Write spaced letters when needed: `C-B-I`, `E-D`, `O-T-P`, `I-V-R`.
4. **Breath & Pacing:**
   - Maximum 8 to 12 words per sentence.
   - Use em-dashes (`—`) and ellipsis (`...`) for natural dramatic pauses (0.15s – 0.25s).

## 📋 5. THE 10-POINT SCRIPT AUDIT MATRIX

Before approving any script for voiceover generation, run this 10-point checklist:
1. [ ] Does the first sentence create an immediate personal curiosity loop?
2. [ ] Is there zero generic AI throat-clearing?
3. [ ] Does Act 2 contain at least ONE sticky, memorable soundbite?
4. [ ] Does Act 3 reveal a non-obvious hidden mechanism or syndicate?
5. [ ] Are all statistics verified by at least two independent credible sources?
6. [ ] Is the total word count strictly between 110–125 words for Shorts?
7. [ ] Does measured TTS duration land between 40.0s and 44.5s (strictly < 50s)?
8. [ ] Are phone numbers, numbers, and acronyms phonetically calibrated?
9. [ ] Are SFX cues explicitly marked for every visual transition?
10. [ ] Is there an actionable safety protocol and a crisp Benaqaab India Subscribe CTA in the final 8 seconds?
