## PART 3 — WHAT **NEVER** TO ADD 🚫

### Absolute bans (channel-killer tier)

| Never add | Why |
|---|---|
| ❌ Unlicensed broadcast / news / MEA / movie / sports clips — "filter or no filter" | Copyright strikes. The filter made it worse (circumvention); without it, it's just straight infringement |
| ❌ ANY edit made **to hide content from Content ID** (halftone-for-evasion, mirroring, cropping-for-evasion, pitch/speed change on audio, tiny zooms, border padding) | ToS circumvention → can terminate ALL your channels |
| ❌ Commercial/trending songs on anime edits & shorts ("AMV style" is the trap) | Claims/strikes from labels. Modified versions of them = circumvention stacking |
| ❌ Content from terminated/restricted creators, or reuploads of anything YouTube removed | Explicitly "circumvention" per official policy |
| ❌ Real person appearing to say/do something they didn't (deepfake), UNLESS flagged with the Studio checkbox + on-screen label | Synthetic media policy |
| ❌ Copied scripts read verbatim from news sites/blogs | "Inauthentic content" — kills monetization |

### Soft bans (reputation / review-risk tier)

| Never add | Why |
|---|---|
| ⛔ Internal pack text: `High-CTR Titles`, `Thumbnail Blueprint`, `=== TECHNICAL SPECS ===`, A/B variants | Looks manipulative; pollutes descriptions |
| ⛔ 30+ hashtag walls | Spam signal |
| ⛔ Keyword-jam in titles (`#shorts UPI MDR 15 oct`) | Reads bot-ish; write for humans |
| ⛔ Unnamed severe accusations ("terrorist", "radicalized", "scammer" at real people) without linked source | Misinformation + defamation + harassment review |
| ⛔ Fake "LIVE" / "BREAKING NOW" framing on dated roundups | Misleading metadata |
| ⛔ AI photoreal images of real events WITHOUT "AI-generated" on-screen label | You nailed this on the currency note video — keep that standard |
| ⛔ "Guaranteed profit" / tip-giving / price predictions in finance content | YPP "AI persona + finance advice" bucket + SEBI-adjacent risk |
| ⛔ Medical advice framing ("ye khaaiye theek ho jayega") | Health misinformation bucket |
| ⛔ Election outcome claims / unverified voter-suppression framing | Election misinformation = strike-strict territory. Stick to documented process + official links (your SIR video did this perfectly ✅) |
| ⛔ Dead links / "source file linked above" references that don't exist | Sloppy + looks deceptive |
