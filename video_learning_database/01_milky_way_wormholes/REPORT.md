## 6. EDITING, CUTS, TRANSITIONS & EFFECTS FORENSICS

### Cuts & Cut Frequency
- **Average Shot Length (ASL):** **3.8 seconds**. Never allows a single visual to hold static for more than 4.5 seconds.
- **Cutting Archetypes:**
  - *J-Cut (Audio Lead-in):* Voiceover explanation of the next concept starts 0.3s before the visual cut occurs. Creates seamless mental anticipation.
  - *Punch Zoom Cut:* Jump from Medium Shot to Close-up on the host when delivering counter-intuitive facts (e.g., *"Voyager 1 abhi tak Solar System se bahar nahi nikli!"*).
  - *Match-Position Scale Morph:* When transitioning from Solar System to Galaxy, the Sun shrinks down to a tiny dot in the exact screen center where the galaxy core subsequently expands.

### Transitions
- **Seamless Motion Blur Whip-Pan:** Used when shifting between different orders of magnitude (Planets ➔ Stars ➔ Galaxy).
- **Camera Push-Through / Warp Tunnel:** Smooth spatial transition through black hole / wormhole throat.
- **Zero Cheap Transitions:** No star wipes, page curls, or basic slide transitions. Every transition is motivated by camera momentum or light intensity.

### Visual Effects & Motion Primitives
- **Spacetime Mesh Grid:** Procedural 3D wireframe plane mapped with gravitational depression wells.
- **Metric Pill Badge:** High-contrast yellow pill (`#FFE600`) with clean bold dark typography.
- **Volumetric Backlight Glow:** Soft radial gradient bloom layered between the chroma-keyed host and the background plate.
- **Continuous Z-Space Drift:** Subtle 1.02× slow push-in applied to all static diagrams to prevent frame freeze.
