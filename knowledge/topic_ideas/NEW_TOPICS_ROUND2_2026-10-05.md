## 3. ARTEMIS II — humans back at the Moon after 1972
**Hook:** "50 saal baad pehli baar insaan Chaand ke paas pahunche — aur raaste me toilet bhi theek karni padi."

**Verified numbers (mission already flown — zero risk):** launched **1 April 2026, 22:35 UTC** from Kennedy LC-39B; **9 days 1 h 32 m**; splashdown **10–11 April**; first crewed flight beyond low Earth orbit since **Apollo 17 (1972)** and the only non-Apollo crewed flight beyond LEO ever; first crewed flight of SLS and Orion.

**The four crew + firsts:** Reid Wiseman (commander, oldest human beyond LEO), Victor Glover (pilot, first person of colour on a lunar mission), **Christina Koch** (record 328-day single spaceflight by a woman; fixed the Orion toilet in flight), Jeremy Hansen (CSA — first non-American beyond LEO). Capsule named **Integrity**; launch mass 78,000 lb / 35,000 kg.

**Drama worth animating:** two scrubs before launch — a liquid-hydrogen leak in February, a helium flow issue in March that forced a rollback to the VAB — then success in April. Programme cost to date: **$93 B (2012–2025)**.

**Animation plan:** Earth→Moon to true scale (people are always shocked), the free-return figure-8, "farthest humans have ever been" marker on the map, then the crew day-by-day. Close on Artemis III (landing) — that's where the story goes next.

**Sources:** https://www.nasa.gov/mission/artemis-ii/ · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis_II

## 8. OCTOBER SKY — Halley's dust at 66 km/s
**Hook:** "Is mahine aasman me Halley's comet ki dhool — 66 kilometre per second ki speed se."

**Verified dates:** **Draconids** active Oct 6–10, peak **Oct 8–9** — from comet 21P/Giacobini-Zinner, ~5–10/hour, and the 2026 peak falls near a **new moon** (dark skies). **Orionids** active Oct 2–Nov 7, peak **Oct 21–22** pre-dawn — debris of **Halley's Comet**, ~20/hour, entry speed **66 km/s (41 mi/s)**, trails lasting seconds to minutes. Bonus: **Saturn at opposition Oct 4** (brightest this year), Moon with Mars & Jupiter Oct 5–6, **Moon near Saturn Oct 24**, **Hunter's Moon Oct 25–26**. Comet 161P/Hartley–IRAS closest to Earth Oct 2 (magnitude ~11.3 — telescope only).

**Mechanism:** Earth crosses Halley's orbit **twice a year** (May and October) → two showers from one comet. Show the orbit crossing, then the radiant point, then why 66 km/s is ~200× a rifle bullet.

**Timing:** publish before **8 Oct** for Draconids, before **21 Oct** for Orionids. Two films, one asset pack.

**Sources:** https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/space-astronomy/astronomy/night-sky-highlights-october-2026 · https://astrobackyard.com/october-2026-astronomy/
