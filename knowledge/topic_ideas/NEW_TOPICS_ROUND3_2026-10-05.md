## 8. QUANTUM'S THRESHOLD YEAR — ⚠️ verify hard before using
**Hook:** "Qubit ke bina duniya ka ganit adhoora tha — ab machine galtiyan khud theek kar rahi hai."

**Solid, verifiable anchors only:** Google's **Willow** chip (105 physical qubits) demonstrated **below-threshold error correction** — adding qubits *reduces* errors (published in Nature); IBM went past **1,000 physical qubits** with Condor and shifted focus to quality with the Heron line; IBM's **120-qubit Nighthawk** targets verified quantum advantage by **end-2026**; Microsoft reported sustained **topological** qubit-pair operation in early 2026.
