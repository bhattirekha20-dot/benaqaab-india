## TOPIC 1 — RBI rate decision and your EMI  ⭐ RECOMMENDED  *(time-critical)*

**Hook:** "Kal aapki EMI badh sakti hai — aur ek bada kaaran hai: baarish."
**Angle:** one chain reaction — weak monsoon → food prices → CPI above target → RBI pulls the rate
lever → your EMI and your savings both change. Nobody sees the chain; we animate it.

**Verified numbers (with dates and sources)**
| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MPC meeting | 5–7 Oct 2026; decision announced **7 Oct** | Business Standard poll, 1 Oct 2026 |
| Current repo rate | **5.25 %**, unchanged in the last four reviews | BS / Reuters |
| Expected action | **+25 bps → 5.50 %**; Reuters poll 35 of 61 economists (18–28 Sep); BS poll 8 of 10 | ET/Reuters 28 Sep 2026 |
| Why | CPI **4.82 %** in Aug vs 4.45 % in Jul — third straight month above the 4 % target | Whalesbook 4 Oct 2026 |
| Global driver | Brent crude **above $100/bbl**; rupee near **96/USD**, about **−6 % this year** | Whalesbook, ET/Reuters |
| First hike since | **February 2023** | Reuters poll |
| What comes next | Union Bank: one or two more hikes in FY27 → repo to **5.75–6.00 %**; Reuters: rates seen at 5.75 % until at least mid-2028 — "**one of the shallowest hiking cycles in at least a decade**" | Times Now 4 Oct; ET/Reuters |
| Governor | Sanjay Malhotra; August minutes showed several members favouring a hike if inflation broadened | ET/Reuters |
| The rain link | 2026 monsoon ended **12.6 % below normal** — driest in 13 years; 43 % of districts 20–72 % below normal | Bhaskar English, 1 Oct 2026 |
| Dissent | Bank of Baroda's Madan Sabnavis expects **no change** (hike before festival season won't help deposit transmission) | BS poll |

**Honesty requirement:** until 7 Oct this is an *expectation*, not a fact. Label every forward
number "expected / polled". After the announcement, update to the actual decision and quote the
statement. The film must work either way — write the VO so the decision line is one swappable beat.

**Animation plan (mechanism, not slides):** monsoon cloud → rain-deficit map of India → mandi
price cards → CPI dial crossing 4 % → RBI lever being pulled in the middle of a balance beam →
EMI bar for a ₹50 lakh, 20-year loan at 8.50 % vs the same loan after +25 bps → savings/FD rate
ticking the other way → countdown to 7 Oct. End on the open question: "aapki EMI kitni badhegi?"

**Risk:** if we publish after 7 Oct without updating, the film is wrong. That is a one-line fix —
swap the "expected" beat for the real number and the statement's wording.

## TOPIC 2 — Gaganyaan: India's first robot astronaut goes first

**Hook:** "Insaan se pehle robot jaayega — India ka pehla crewed space mission aise test hota hai."
**Angle:** the three-step ladder to putting an Indian in orbit, and what actually has to pass.

**Verified numbers**
| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| First uncrewed mission | targeted **Q4 2026 (Oct–Dec)**, will carry **Vyommitra**, a half-humanoid | Policy Edge, 6 Aug 2026; idrw/TOI, Sep 2026 |
| Ladder | **three uncrewed missions** before the crewed flight, crewed targeted **2028** | ISRO chief V Narayanan; Wikipedia status |
| Money | **₹20,193 crore** approved Sept 2024 for eight missions under the human-spaceflight programme | Policy Edge |
| Ground work | **8,000+ ground tests** done by early 2026; all structural, engineering and propulsion qualification complete | Wikipedia, Sep 2026 |
| Engine margin | CE-20 cryogenic engine hot-fired **8,810 s** vs the 6,350 s minimum required for human rating | Wikipedia |
| Already flown | **TV-D1** validated the crew escape system in flight; two air-drop tests validated the parachute/deceleration sequence; **TV-D2** next | Policy Edge; Wikipedia |
| Recovery | splashdown in the **Arabian Sea** chosen over the Bay of Bengal; **48 backup locations** in international waters; up-righting cold-gas system for the capsule | Wikipedia |
| Partners | ESA (tracking network), Australian Space Agency (crew recovery), CNES (flight surgeons), Roscosmos (training) | Policy Edge |
| Beyond | first space-station module **BAS-01** approved; five-module **Bharatiya Antariksh Station by 2035** | Policy Edge |

**Honesty requirement:** the launch date is a **window**, not a date — say "targeted Q4 2026".
Do not say "will launch"; say "targeted".

**Animation plan:** a three-rung ladder drawn to scale (uncrewed ×3 → crewed) → Vyommitra drawn
as a simple half-humanoid on a schematic seat → the escape-system test path from TV-D1 (pad →
abort → parachute → sea) → an Arabian Sea map with the 48 backup sites as a scatter → a
₹20,193-crore block against nothing else → closing question: "2026 ke aakhir tak?"

**Risk:** window may slip inside the window; frame everything as targets and keep the credit
"as of" dated on screen.

## TOPIC 3 — The driest monsoon in 13 years, and how it is declared "over"

**Hook:** "Is saal baarish ka record tuta — 13 saal me sabse kam. Par 'monsoon khatam' kaun declare karta hai?"
**Angle:** two halves — the season's numbers, then the exact three-condition test IMD uses before
it will announce withdrawal. A rules topic with a live weather story attached.

**Verified numbers**
| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Season deficit, final | **12.6 % below normal** — 13th driest monsoon on record, lowest in 11 years | Bhaskar English, 1 Oct 2026 |
| Deficit at withdrawal start | **14.9 %** as of the third week of September — show both, with dates | Mathrubhumi, 20 Sep 2026 |
| Regional split | NE India **lowest since 1901**; **Odisha +30 %**; 14 states including Bihar, Punjab, Haryana above 20 % deficit; **43 % of districts 20–72 % below** | Bhaskar; Mathrubhumi |
| Withdrawal test | rainfall stopped for **5 continuous days** + an **anticyclone at 850 hPa** established + considerable **reduction in moisture** (satellite + tephigrams) — only checked after 1 September | HT explainer, 21 Sep 2026 |
| Timeline | withdrawal began from west Rajasthan 19–20 Sep; **full withdrawal expected by ~10 Oct**; northeast monsoon likely **after 15 Oct** | Mathrubhumi; IMD; Bhaskar |
| Climate drivers | strong **El Niño** held through the season; a developing **positive IOD** worked the other way | HT explainer |
| What is next | ISRO forecast **three cyclones in the North Indian Ocean for Oct–Dec** — verify the "83 % higher strength" phrasing before it goes on screen | Mathrubhumi headline |

**Honesty requirement:** the two deficit figures (14.9 % mid-September vs 12.6 % for the season)
must both appear with their dates — this is exactly the "show the disagreement" rule, and here it
is a moving number, not a contradiction.

**Animation plan:** an India map filling with deficit shading → the withdrawal line sweeping as an
arc while three checkboxes tick (5 dry days / 850 hPa anticyclone / moisture drop) → the heat low
collapsing in cross-section → cyclone season opening over the Bay → closing question: "agla monsoon
kab theek hoga?"

**Risk:** cyclone forecast detail needs verification before use; drop that line if it does not hold.
