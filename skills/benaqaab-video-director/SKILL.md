# 🎬 Benaqaab Video Director & Production Supervisor

## 1. Locked Production Order of Operations
```
1. Read MEMORY.md and project directives.
2. Formulate script & hook in Hinglish (built around ONE central investigative question).
3. Two-source research validation (verify facts, figures, and dates).
4. Audio / VO Generation & Duration Measurement (mutagen audio inspection is the master clock).
5. Code-based Scene Assembly (HTML5/Canvas/CSS components).
6. 1-GATE HTML APPROVAL: Present interactive HTML proof to user. Wait for approval.
7. Render full MP4 (using imageio-ffmpeg / browser capture).
8. Render companion vertical Short (<50s).
9. High-impact thumbnail generation + YouTube metadata pack.
```

## 2. Long-Form Documentary Rules (Max 5 Minutes)
- **Target Duration:** 3:30 to 4:30 (absolute maximum 5:00).
- **Cold Open (0:00 - 0:20):** Plunge directly into the mystery or scandal. No channel intro sting. Promise clearly stated within 20 seconds.
- **Presenter Screen Time:** Under 20% total runtime. Host appears for:
  1. The opening hook (≤8s).
  2. 1–2 key reveals or verdicts.
  3. The final outro CTA.
  All other footage must be full-screen evidence, motion graphics, and archival context.
- **Voiceover Rule:** Male voice for long-form, female voice for Shorts. Hinglish narration with English on-screen titles.
- **End-of-Video CTA:** Mandatory verbal + visual Like ★ and Subscribe ▶ call to action in the final 5–8 seconds.
